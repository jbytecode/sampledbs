\section{About the notes}

These notes cover the basics of SQL using the Northwind database in SQLite.
This document is intended to provide a quick reference for SQL queries and operations, 
and is used in my 1st year information technologies course at Istanbul University. 
The notes include examples of SELECT, UPDATE, DELETE, and INSERT INTO statements, 
as well as the use of the LIMIT clause and various filtering techniques.
The notes also include limited examples of JOIN operations and summary statistics using some 
aggregate functions (e.g., COUNT, SUM, AVG).

\section{LIMIT}

The \texttt{LIMIT} clause is used to specify the maximum number of records to return from a query. 
It is often used in conjunction with the \texttt{SELECT} statement to limit the number of rows returned.\index{LIMIT}


\section{Comments}

In SQL, we can define single-line comments using two hyphens (\texttt{--}).\index{Comments}

\section{UPDATE}

Description: Updates the unit price values of the products 
with product IDs 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 by multiplying their current unit price values by 1.25.

\subsection{Update Unit Prices of Specific Products}
\begin{verbatim}

SET UnitPrice = UnitPrice * 1.25

\end{verbatim}\index{UPDATE}\index{SET}\index{WHERE}\index{IN}

\subsection{Increase Units in Stock of Specific Products}

\section{Exercises}

Suppose that Prices table contains the following fields: ProductID, UnitPrice, and UnitsInStock.
Here is the table data:

\begin{table}[H]
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
ProductID	& UnitPrice	& UnitsInStock \\
\hline
1	& 10	& 100 \\
2	& 20	& 200 \\
3	& 30	& 300 \\
4	& 40	& 400 \\
5	& 50	& 500 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Write SQL queries to perform the following operations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Calculate the total value of the inventory (UnitPrice multiplied by UnitsInStock for each product, and then summed across all products).
    \item Find the average unit price of the products in stock.
    \item List the products that have a unit price greater than 25 and are in stock.
    \item Update the unit price of all products by increasing it by 10%.
    \item Delete the products that have a unit price less than 15.
\end{itemize}

Answers:

\begin{verbatim}
-- Total value of the inventory
SELECT SUM(UnitPrice * UnitsInStock) AS TotalInventoryValue
FROM Prices;
\end{verbatim}\index{SELECT}\index{SUM}

\begin{verbatim}
-- Average unit price of the products in stock
SELECT AVG(UnitPrice) AS AverageUnitPrice
FROM Prices
WHERE UnitsInStock > 0;
\end{verbatim}\index{SELECT}\index{AVG}\index{WHERE}

\begin{verbatim}
-- List products with unit price greater than 25 and in stock
SELECT ProductID, UnitPrice, UnitsInStock
FROM Prices
WHERE UnitPrice > 25 AND UnitsInStock > 0;
\end{verbatim}\index{SELECT}\index{WHERE}\index{AND}

\begin{verbatim}
-- Update unit price by increasing it by 10%
UPDATE Prices
SET UnitPrice = UnitPrice * 1.10;
\end{verbatim}\index{UPDATE}\index{SET}

\begin{verbatim}
-- Delete products with unit price less than 15
DELETE FROM Prices
WHERE UnitPrice < 15;
\end{verbatim}\index{DELETE}\index{WHERE}
